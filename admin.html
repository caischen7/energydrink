    04 CANDIDATES

      ! HOW TO READ THIS MAP
      • Vacancy tags are stale. Treat them as leads, never inventory.
      • Competitor ≠ bad. Zero restaurants nearby often means zero reason to be there.
      • Signal ≠ access. A divided arterial can still mean no left-in/left-out — stand on the curb.
      • Sparse map ≠ sparse market. OSM completeness varies block to block.
      • Straight-line radius lies. Rivers, rail and highways cut real catchments.
      • A parking polygon isn't your parking. Verify in the lease.
      • Stop count ≠ ridership. A 4-bus-a-day stop scores like a BRT station.
