# Founder Advisory Board — 12 seats

A standing board of consumer-beverage and energy-drink operators assembled to
pressure-test the **Market Intelligence** tool and decide what it should help a
founder do: *find a defensible white space for a new drink.* Personas are
composites — archetypes that map to real plays in the category — chosen so that
every major lens on the market is represented around the table.

## How each seat reads "opportunity"

- **Maya / Dev / Sofia / Tariq / Marcus / Helena** — *formulation-led*: a new
  drink wins on an unmet functional benefit (protein, hydration, gut, focus,
  adaptogen, clean energy). They lean on the tool's **demand-vs-supply gap**
  engine.
- **Grace / Priya / Jordan** — *brand/audience-led*: a new drink wins on who
  it's for and how it shows up. They lean on **share-of-voice**, social
  engagement, and pain-point language they can weaponize in copy.
- **Sam / Nina** — *reality check*: distribution velocity and unit economics
  decide whether a white space is actually a *business*. They push for data the
  tool doesn't have yet.
- **Andre** — *methodology*: keeps the scoring honest and flags where scraped
  signal will mislead.
