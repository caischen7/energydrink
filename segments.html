// HOW SEGMENTS WERE ASSIGNED

RULE-BASED. The dataset has no "need state" field. Each SKU is assigned by a documented rule set over brand, PRODUCT_TYPE, SUB_PRODUCT_TYPE and the product description — see data/scripts/add_segments.py. This is an authored judgement, not a measurement, and reasonable people would place some brands differently.

ORDER MATTERS. Positioning beats formulation: Celsius and Bang are both sugar-free, but they sit in Wellness and Fitness because that is who they are sold to. "Zero-Sugar Mainstream" therefore holds only the zero variants of classic brands.

SALES BASIS. Box area and share use trailing-12-month revenue from the PDI convenience-store panel. Under 0.4% of sales fall in "Other / Niche", so the map covers essentially the whole measured category.

CHANNEL CAVEAT. Convenience stores only — no club, grocery or e-commerce. Segments that over-index in those channels (wellness especially) are understated here relative to their true national size.
