// METHODOLOGY

SOURCES. Amazon product + review scrape, YouTube video metadata + comment corpus, Instagram brand posts. Cleaned into /data/{amazon,instagram,youtube,combined}.

PIPELINE. data/scripts/build_dashboard_json.py reduces 129K+ records (incl. a 25 MB comment file) to a 10 KB aggregate — nothing large is shipped to the browser.

CAVEATS. Share-of-voice uses fractional attribution (a video's views split across the brands it names) and strips music/entertainment false-matches — ~1.2B phantom views (e.g. Eminem's "The Monster") removed. Trends use trailing-12-month windows and exclude the partial final month. Attention ≠ sales; Instagram is a 15-post-per-brand sample.

SO WHAT. White space sits at premium price + top quality, and a no-crash angle targets an under-discussed theme — though that's mention frequency, not yet sentiment. Where ION is positioned.
